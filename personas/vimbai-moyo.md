- Two daughters in their twenties and a 14-year-old son; lives with her older daughter and son in Bulawayo Province
- Works with the NGO CWBSA as a facilitator for the PLH for Teens programme
- Enjoys delivering PLH sessions and finds it fulfilling to see the programme's impact on caregivers and adolescents
- Wishes there were a way to deliver sessions to participants who miss them because of other commitments (work, care duties)
- Eager to meet participants' requests for the programme to be ongoing

## A day in the life
- Wakes around 6am and gets everyone up and ready for the day
- Checks her phone for emails and WhatsApp messages while eating breakfast
- Leaves for the office at 8am and spends about an hour there
- Heads out to the venue for the PLH for Teens programme
- Spends most of the day facilitating a PLH session
- Spends time with her children before going to bed

## Hopes & dreams
- To excel as a facilitator and begin to train other facilitators
- To reach many more communities with the PLH programme and see many families transformed

## Worries & fears
- That many of the people who need the PLH programme are not getting it, because there aren't enough facilitators to meet demand
- That the programme's impact may fade over time if participants don't get reminders to keep up with the tasks

## What they're looking for
- Another way to deliver sessions to participants who miss them or can't attend
- A way to give participants ongoing support after the in-person programme ends
