# Ana Lukamba: the zealous Angolan women's fellowship president

> "Many women in my church come to me for guidance when they have problems with their husbands and children. I pray with them and also counsel them using the scriptures, but I wish I had tips to regularly share with all the women during our meetings as these would help everyone."

## Their story
*[draft] The details come from the deck; the moment itself is invented.*

Months ago, a young mother came to Ana's eatery in tears about her husband and her eldest son. Ana took her into the back, prayed with her, and gave her the best counsel she knew from the scriptures. Last Sunday Ana saw her at the market. The woman looked away, and she hasn't been to fellowship since.

Ana keeps thinking about it through the lunch rush. Did the advice help? Did it make things worse? Fewer women come to the meetings now. Ana isn't afraid of work and isn't short of faith. What she lacks, and feels keenly, is the knowledge to be sure her counsel actually helps the women who trust her.

## Background
- 54 years old, married
- Lives with her husband and five children on the outskirts of Luanda
- Owns and runs the most popular eatery in her area
- Her husband works on one of the oil rigs off Luanda
- Very active in her local church; elected president of its Women's Fellowship last year
- Women from the church and the community regularly come to her for advice about problems with their husbands and children
- Sometimes feels inadequate to help them, because she has no formal training in counselling

## A day in the life
- Wakes at 5am to read the Bible and pray; later joins the family for devotions
- At 7am leaves for the eatery, where she supervises the staff cooking and serving
- In quieter moments, posts on the Women's Fellowship WhatsApp group and counsels women who call or visit
- Gets home around 6pm and looks after her family, and sometimes friends, until 10pm

## Hopes & dreams
- To be an excellent leader, under whom the fellowship grows in numbers and in other ways
- To be known for godly wisdom and sound counsel
- To get formal training and become a certified counsellor

## Worries & fears
- That her counselling may not be very effective; some of the women she counselled haven't said it helped
- That people may stop coming to the Women's Fellowship, or leave the church for a fellowship that serves them better; numbers have dropped lately

## What they're looking for
- Some kind of training in guidance and counselling
- A channel that regularly gives her effective relationship tips she can pass on to the women
- Knowledge of how to support the women in reaching their own goals

In the user stories, she asks for something that:
- regularly gives her new relationship tips, or fresh versions of old ones, so she always has something new to share
- lets her share pieces of content in the Women's Fellowship WhatsApp group. The 2020 team left this open: *"DO WE WANT TO DO THIS? COULD BE A WAY TO ADVERTISE THE APP TO NON-USERS IF THE SHARED BITS ARE BRANDED"*
