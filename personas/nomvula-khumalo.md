# Nomvula Khumalo: the overstretched Durban social worker

> [draft] "I have more open files than I can count. If the app could tell me which parents finished the course, I could close the files of the ones who are trying and spend my time on the children who aren't safe."

## Their story
*[draft] Invented.*

Nomvula has a children's court hearing on Thursday. The magistrate will ask whether the mother has completed a parenting programme. The mother says she has been going. Nomvula mostly believes her, but belief isn't evidence.

At lunch, eating over a file, she hears from a colleague that some families in the district are using a parenting app linked to a local programme. If the app could show who finished the sessions, she could send the court a printout, close the files of parents who are trying, and spend her time where children are in real danger. She has seen what happens when a social worker misses something. What she carries is the fear of the child she didn't get to in time.

## Worries & fears
*[draft]*

## What they're looking for
*[draft]*
- A parenting programme she can refer families to, that works
- Proof that a parent attended or completed it
- Early warning of which families are struggling
- A way to make parenting support part of a family's care plan

## Where we'd lose them
