# Olusegun Adeyemi: the successful Nigerian banker

> "I love my family and would love to have a close relationship with my children but that seems impossible. As they get older, they no longer want to involve me in their lives and this leaves me angry and frustrated!"

## Their story
*[draft] The details come from the deck; the moment itself is invented.*

On the second evening of the family holiday abroad, Olusegun took everyone to a restaurant he had chosen months before. All three children sat with their earphones in. He told them to take them out; his son rolled his eyes; Olusegun raised his voice until the people at the next table turned round. The children took the earphones out and said nothing for the rest of the meal, which was worse.

In the hotel room he lay awake thinking about his own father, who never once asked him how he was, and about the promise he made himself to be different. He would never say this to his friends at the golf club. He isn't sure he could say it to his wife. If help exists, it has to be somewhere nobody will see him looking for it.

## Background
- 52 years old, married
- Lives in Lagos with his wife and three teenage children
- Works as a banking executive
- Never had a good relationship with his father, and always dreamed of being the ideal father
- The last two holidays abroad were a disaster: he kept yelling at the children for having their earphones on, and they withdrew from him even more
- Always thought parenting was a woman's job, but isn't sure any more
- Feels terrible that his children now avoid being in the same room as him

## A day in the life
- Leaves for work at 7am and spends most of the day in his office
- Plays golf after work most evenings
- Gets home late in the evening and watches TV until bed
- Thinks about what his children do online on their iPhones
- [draft] The realistic window for our tech is late evening, alone, on his own phone

## Hopes & dreams
- A close, strong relationship with all his children
- A loving, peaceful atmosphere at home
- To be a friend and confidant to his children

## Worries & fears
- His relationship with his children getting worse
- Becoming the kind of man his own father was: distant and unapproachable
- His children falling in with the wrong crowd and not turning out well; his son has started keeping late nights
- That his children won't visit or call him once they leave home

## What they're looking for
- A way to build a closer relationship with his children
- How to resolve conflicts with them without yelling
- Parenting advice he can get without asking family or friends, because that "isn't manly"

In the user stories, he asks for something that:
- lets him use a username he chooses, with his real name hidden from other users
- keeps his phone number and email private unless he chooses to share them
- has a simple interface, and opens with one tap
- arranges tips by topic, and is evidence-based
- reminds him to praise his children
- is engaging and not wordy, so it feels relaxing to use
- tracks his own use (time spent, sessions viewed, posts) and what he practised and what changed
- sets rules against bullying, threats and indecent posts, which users must read before they start
- has no ads
- he can share with friends and family
