# Lucía Herrera: the loyal London-based programme officer

> [draft] "I believe in this work. Help me defend it. Give me honest numbers early, including the bad ones, and never let my board surprise me."

## Their story
*[draft] Invented.*

It's board week. Lucía's director asks why the PLH Digital pilot reports 12,000 "users" when the partner's own slide says 900 families completed the programme. Lucía doesn't know which number is real. She spends the night emailing across three time zones and gets the answer: 12,000 downloads, most of them from one social media advert. The 900 is the number that matters.

She explains it to the board, and they accept it, but she now wonders what else she hasn't been told. Her mother ran a parenting group in their neighbourhood in Medellín; Lucía knows what this work can do. What she carries is real belief in the programme, and the exposure of being the person who vouched for it.

*[draft]*

- Assurance on safeguarding and data protection
- Stories of change, shared with the families' consent
