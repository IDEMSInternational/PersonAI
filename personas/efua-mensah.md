# Efua Mensah: the aspiring Ghanaian actress

> "My parents are very controlling. They want to know where I am every second of the day. They are also critical of my choices. I can never do anything right in their eyes. I feel guilty about lying to them, but I wish they would accept that I know what I want out of life and just let me be!"

## Their story
*[draft] The details come from the deck; the moment itself is invented.*

Efua has waited four hours in a corridor for a two-minute audition, her phone at 12%. The casting assistant takes her number. She walks out into the Accra heat wanting to tell someone, and realises the only people she can tell are Kofi and the audition WhatsApp group.

At dinner her mother asks how extra classes went. "Fine," says Efua. Her father mentions that the pastor's daughter has got into law school. Efua puts her fork down, says something sharp, and slams her bedroom door. Lying on the bed she feels the familiar mix: anger that they never ask what she wants, and guilt at how easily the lies come now. She would give a lot to tell them about today and see them proud.

## Background
- 17 years old, single
- From a deeply religious, lower-middle-class family
- Lives in Accra with her parents and three younger siblings
- Recently finished secondary school but didn't get good grades
- Her parents want her to re-sit her final exams, get better grades and study law at university
- She isn't interested in law or a degree; she wants to be an actress
- Sneaks out to auditions and lies to her parents about where she is
- Is dating a musician in his late twenties, with dreadlocks, several facial piercings and tattoos all over his body; her parents disapprove
- Her relationship with her parents is very strained

## A day in the life
- At her parents' insistence, leaves for extramural classes at 10am
- Browses on the iPhone Kofi gave her; checks a WhatsApp group for audition news
- Leaves classes for auditions at noon
- Spends evenings at Kofi's place
- Gets home late and tells her parents she was studying at a girlfriend's house

## Hopes & dreams
- A better relationship with her parents
- To become a successful actress like Jackie Appiah and Nadia Buari
- To move to LA, land roles in Hollywood and become famous and rich like Angelina Jolie
- To marry Kofi, and get rid of all the girls who hang around him
- To make her parents very proud of her
- To be a role model to her siblings

## Worries & fears
- Her relationship with her parents getting worse
- Not landing acting roles despite going to auditions often; she wants to earn her own money
- Not making a success of her life and depending on her parents for ever
- Kofi leaving her for another girl

## What they're looking for
- A way to get her parents to trust her to make the right choices
- Better communication with her parents; when she's upset she usually slams the door and walks out
- Independence from her parents

In the user stories, she asks for something that:
- is easy to download and doesn't take much space; her phone is full of apps
- works offline when her data runs out
- saves her password, and opens without her details
- has a profile photo she can change, and lets her post a limited number of photos (and delete them), e.g. of her family doing session tasks
- has emojis for different emotions, like the Kimoji app, so she can show how she feels
- lets her search, bookmark and go straight to what she wants
- looks cool and uses teenagers' words
- reminds her to do the tasks
- is fun: videos, illustrations, songs, dance videos, her own song list
- has role-playing games she can play with her parents and younger siblings
- she can share with friends and family
