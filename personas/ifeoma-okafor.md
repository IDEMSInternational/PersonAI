# Ifeoma Okafor: the curious Nigerian data science intern

> [draft] "Give me a real problem and some data I'm allowed to touch. I'll give you three months of my best work."

## Their story
*[draft] Invented.*

Ifeoma is in the last months of a data science master's in Accra, on a scholarship. Her supervisor arranges a three-month placement with the PLH Digital team, analysing how families use the app. In week one she gets invitations to the code repository and the team chat, and a message that her access to real data is "still being sorted".

By week four she is still waiting. Her questions sit unanswered in a busy channel, so she invents a small fake dataset and builds a dashboard on it. In week twelve she presents to a half-empty call. Her code isn't merged, and her reference letter takes six weeks to arrive. What she carries is hunger to do something real, and a quiet fear that she was a box someone ticked.
