# Awa Konaté: the ambitious Ivorian ministry digital lead

> [draft] "Nous voulons bien l'adopter, mais chez nous : hébergé ici, en français, et que nos équipes puissent le faire vivre sans appeler l'étranger."
> ("We'd gladly adopt it, but on our terms: hosted here, in French, and run by our own teams without calling abroad.")

## Their story
*[draft] Invented.*

At a regional conference, Awa watches a PLH Digital demo. A short video of a mother and her teenage daughter stays with her all afternoon. Back in Abidjan she asks the questions her minister will ask: where are the servers? Who holds our citizens' data? Who maintains it?

The answers: a cloud server abroad, a small team in another country, English and Swahili only, and no local entity she could contract through. Her country's data protection rules [to confirm] and her minister's insistence on keeping citizens' data at home rule it out. She files it under "later", with regret. What she carries is a real wish to give families something good, and the job of answering for every risk to a minister and a parliament.

## Hopes & dreams
*[draft]*
- National digital services for families that actually work
- A team that builds its own skills rather than depending on foreign consultants
- A showcase project for her minister

## Worries & fears
*[draft]*
- Citizens' data leaving the country
- Being locked in to a foreign supplier
- A project that collapses when the donor leaves and lands on her budget
- A scandal with political consequences

## What they're looking for
*[draft]*
- A way to host the app and its data in the country
- All content in French
- Code and content her team can own, change and run
- Handover documentation and training for her team
- A clear statement of what personal data is collected, where it is stored and for how long
- A local partner her ministry can contract through

## Context & constraints
*[draft]*
- **Device:** ministry laptop, smartphone
- **Connectivity & data cost:** good in the Abidjan office
- **Apps they already use:** email, WhatsApp, Word, government procurement systems
- **Language(s) & literacy:** French; English for donor meetings
- **Time available:** decisions move slowly; budgets are annual
- **Who supports them / who they support:** the minister's office, her technical team, donors

## Where we'd lose them
*[draft]*
- Data that can only be hosted abroad
- No French
- Dependence on a foreign team for every change
- Unclear licences for what a government may change and redistribute

## Test questions
*[draft]*
- Can the app and its data be hosted on servers in-country, with documentation a government team can follow?
- Is all the content available in French?
- Can her team update content and release new versions without the original developers?
- Is there a document listing what personal data is collected, where it is stored and for how long, that her legal team can check against national law?
- Do the code and content licences say clearly what a government may change and redistribute?
